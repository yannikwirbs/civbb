# Code of conduct

CIVBB follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

Be respectful, assume good faith, and keep discussion about the work. Harassment, personal attacks and discriminatory language are not accepted in issues, pull requests, commits or any other project space.

## Reporting

Report conduct problems privately to the maintainer ([@yannikwirbs](https://github.com/yannikwirbs)) through GitHub's [private reporting](https://github.com/yannikwirbs/civbb/security/advisories/new), or use GitHub's "Report content" option on the comment or issue. Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainer may edit or remove content and block participants who break this code.
